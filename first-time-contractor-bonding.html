Underwriting Scorecard

The “3 C’s” Every Surety Underwriter Scores

Unlike traditional insurance, surety is a credit guarantee. Underwriters evaluate your contracting firm across three pillars before approving a single-job or aggregate bonding limit.

C1

Character

Clean corporate and personal credit, positive trade/supplier references, zero unresolved liens or bond claims, and transparent communication with your broker and underwriter.

C2

Capacity

Relevant project resume, experienced estimators and site superintendents, owned or leased equipment, and historical completion of projects similar in scope and dollar size.

C3

Capital

Working Capital (Current Assets − Current Liabilities) and Tangible Net Worth. As a rule of thumb, sureties look for working capital and equity equal to roughly 10% of your uncompleted bonded backlog (and a Current Ratio of 1.3+ or higher).

CPA Reporting Standards

Financial Statement Tiers (Compilation vs. Review Engagement)

Always confirm the required CPA reporting level with your surety broker before instructing your accountant—a completed Compilation (CSRS 4200) cannot simply be upgraded to a Review Engagement without redoing the engagement.

Facility Tier Typical Bond / Project Size Required CPA Financial Level Typical Underwriting Focus
Licence / Express $10,000 TSBC or AB Prepaid No full CPA financials required Personal credit score, clean licensing history, CGL status.
Emerging Facility Up to ~$250K – $500K Compilation (Notice to Reader / CSRS 4200) Working capital, owner PNW, and comparable unbonded project resume.
Growth Facility $500K – $2M+ Independent CPA Review Engagement + WIP 10% working capital/equity benchmark, WIP over/under-billings, bank line.
Major Public / Civil $5M+ Single / Aggregate Review Engagement or Audited Statements + WIP Multi-year profitability, debt-to-equity ratio, dedicated bank credit facility.

Contractor Questionnaire

Completed profile detailing ownership, key staff resumes, and largest completed jobs.

2–3 Years CPA Financials

Current WIP Schedule

Active jobs schedule showing contract price, billed-to-date, cost-to-complete, and margins.

Common Questions

First-Time Contractor Bonding FAQ

Does it cost money to set up a standby Bid Bond facility before I win a tender?

Typically there is $0 upfront setup cost for qualified standby Bid Bond facilities. Bond premiums generally apply only if you win the contract and issue final Performance and Labour & Material Payment bonds (standalone one-off Bid Bonds may carry a minimum fee).

Can a startup contractor with less than 2 years in business get bonded?

Often yes—for $10,000 licence bonds or smaller $100,000 to $250,000 emerging facilities when the principals bring strong prior industry experience, clean personal credit, and solid liquidity.

Why do sureties require personal indemnity?

Because surety is a credit guarantee rather than traditional loss-pooling insurance, sureties require a corporate and shareholder General Indemnity Agreement (GIA) confirming the company and owners stand behind their contractual obligations.
