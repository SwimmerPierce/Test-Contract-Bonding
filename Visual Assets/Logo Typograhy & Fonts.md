# 1. Typography & Fonts

The typography is designed to be modern, highly legible, and authoritative, using a geometric sans-serif family. To achieve this exact look in your development or design software, you can use the widely available, open-source Google Font Montserrat (or premium alternatives like Futura or Helvetica Neue):

- Primary Text ("CONTRACT BONDING")
  - **Font: Montserrat** (Bold / 700) or Futura (Bold)
  - **Style:** UPPERCASE
  - **Character Spacing (Tracking):** Standard / Slightly Tight (-10 to 0 tracking in design software, or letter-spacing: -0.01em in CSS).
  - *Purpose:* Conveys strength, stability, and professionalism.
- Subtext / Domain extension (".CA" & "INFORMATION & ADVICE")
  - **Font: Montserrat** (Semi-Bold / 600) or (Regular / 400)
  - **Style:** UPPERCASE
  - **Character Spacing (Tracking):** Wide for taglines, normal for .ca. (e.g., letter-spacing: 0.15em or 150 tracking in design software for "INFORMATION & ADVICE").
  - *Purpose*: Creates a clean, open, and modern hierarchy.
