## 3. Regulatory Operation, Level 2 Upgrade & Expanded Bonding Capacity
3.1 **Sole Licensed Operator:** The Brokerage shall operate `contractbonding.ca` under its legal name **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** (and any Council-approved DBA) and ensure full compliance with BC PIPA, Alberta PIPA (including Section 13.1), CASL, the *Financial Products Disclosure Regulation* (B.C. Reg. 573/2004), and applicable rules in all Authorized Brokerage Jurisdictions.  
3.2 **Level 1 Restrictions (`Rule 6(1)(a)` & `Rule 6(1)(c)`):** For so long as the Producer holds a **Level 1 Salesperson** licence, the Producer shall never sign, seal, or execute any surety bond or insurance contract on behalf of an insurer (nor use any Level 2/3 signature stamp) and shall conduct all surety and insurance activities under the direct supervision of the Brokerage's Level 3 Nominee or designated Level 2/3 agent.  
3.3 **Level 2 Licence Upgrade & Expanded Surety Bonding Capacity:** The Parties expressly agree that the Producer's operational role and bonding capacity are intended to scale as the Producer advances in licensing:  
   - (a) **Removal of Level 1 Supervision & Signing Restrictions:** Immediately upon the Producer obtaining a **Level 2 General Insurance Agent** licence (or higher) in good standing, Level 1 supervision and bond-signing restrictions cease to apply to the extent permitted by law;  
   - (b) **Expanded Bonding Authority & Surety Capacity:** Upon obtaining a Level 2 licence (or higher), the Brokerage shall grant the Producer expanded operational, underwriting, and account-servicing capacity to handle complex surety bond facilities directly (rather than handing them off internally), subject only to standard brokerage underwriting guidelines and insurer market limits;  
   - (c) **Attorney-in-Fact (Power of Attorney) Appointments:** Upon the Producer achieving Level 2 licensing, the Brokerage agrees to actively support, sponsor, and nominate the Producer for **Attorney-in-Fact / Power of Attorney** bond-signing appointments with the Brokerage's contracted surety insurers (as eligible under each insurer's underwriting standards) so the Producer can sign, seal, and issue bonds directly; and  
   - (d) **Right to Assume Servicing on Renewals:** Upon upgrading to Level 2 (or obtaining any required provincial licence), the Producer may assume active ongoing servicing of previously handed-off Stream B accounts at renewal, thereby upgrading the Producer's renewal compensation on those accounts from the non-servicing rate (**15%**) to the active-servicing rate (**35%**) per Schedule A.

## 4. Operational Jurisdictions & Asymmetric Provincial Expansion
4.1 **Definitions of Operational Jurisdiction:**  
   - (a) **"Authorized Brokerage Jurisdictions"** means British Columbia and Alberta as of the Effective Date, **plus any additional Canadian province or territory** in which the Brokerage subsequently obtains a corporate or non-resident insurance agency licence over time.  
   - (b) **"Producer Licensed Jurisdictions"** means British Columbia as of the Effective Date, **plus any additional Canadian province or territory** in which the Producer subsequently obtains an individual resident or non-resident insurance licence (including Alberta via interprovincial reciprocity or other provinces over time).

4.2 **Recognition of Different Expansion Timelines:** The Parties acknowledge that the Brokerage and the Producer may gain authority to transact in additional provinces at **different times and rates**. Lead routing, servicing rights, and commission categorization on every `contractbonding.ca` lead are governed dynamically by the three tiers below at the time the business is written or renewed:

   - **Tier 1 — Both Brokerage & Producer Licensed in the Province (`Stream A`):** Where both the Brokerage holds an agency licence and the Producer holds an active individual licence in the client's/project's province (and the Producer works on the deal within their licence level), the Producer works on and services the account directly under **Stream A** (**65% Year 1 / 35% Renewal**).
   - **Tier 2 — Brokerage Licensed, Producer Not Yet Licensed in the Province (`Stream B`):** Where the Brokerage is licensed in the province (e.g., Alberta prior to the Producer's individual Alberta certificate, or any future province the Brokerage enters before the Producer is individually licensed there), the Producer shall **not** personally solicit, quote, or advise on the risk. The lead is immediately routed internally to an appropriately licensed Nexus broker under **Stream B** (**35% Year 1**). If the Producer subsequently becomes individually licensed in that province and actively works on the account by renewal, the renewal split upgrades from **15%** to **35%** per Schedule A.
   - **Tier 3 — Brokerage Not Yet Licensed in the Province (`Stream C`):** Where the Brokerage does not hold an agency licence in the client's/project's province, the lead is referred by **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** to an appropriately licensed external partner brokerage under **Stream C** (**90% of the referral fee** to Producer). If the Brokerage subsequently obtains a corporate/non-resident agency licence in that province, subsequent new leads in that province automatically move in-house under **Tier 1 (Stream A)** or **Tier 2 (Stream B)** depending on whether the Producer holds an individual licence there.

## 5. Mandatory Asset Reversion & M&A Buyout Protections (Assignor Equity Protection)
*Because the Assignor built the Platform Assets and is assigning legal title solely to streamline regulatory licensing and pre-tax corporate marketing deductions, the Parties expressly agree that the following protections are a fundamental condition of the assignment:*

5.1 **Restriction on Third-Party Transfer:** The Brokerage shall **not** sell, assign, pledge, license, or transfer the Platform Assets (`contractbonding.ca`, its web hosting infrastructure, database hosting structure, or source code repository) to any unrelated third party, nor allow any third-party lien to attach thereto, without the Assignor's prior written consent.

5.2 **Trigger Events:** A **"Trigger Event"** occurs if any of the following takes place:  
   - (a) The Brokerage enters into an agreement to sell all or substantially all of its assets, book of business, or controlling shares to an unrelated third party (a **"Change of Control"**);  
   - (b) The Assignor's employment or producer engagement with the Brokerage is terminated without just cause;  
   - (c) The Brokerage ceases active operations, winds down, or loses its general insurance agency licence; or  
   - (d) The Brokerage defaults on Schedule A commission payments for more than 45 days after written notice.

5.3 **Option A — Automatic Reversion Right ($1.00):** Upon any Trigger Event (unless the Assignor elects Option B below in writing in a Change of Control transaction), **100% legal and beneficial title to `contractbonding.ca` and the Web Application source code repository (excluding confidential client policy underwriting files required by law to remain with the Brokerage) shall automatically revert and be re-assigned to Chase Addison for $1.00 CAD**. Within **10 business days** of written election by the Assignor, the Brokerage shall execute all CIRA registrant transfer authorizations and infrastructure/repository transfers required to return full control to the Assignor.

5.4 **Option B — M&A Buyout Payout Upon Third-Party Sale:** If a Change of Control occurs and the third-party buyer wishes to retain `contractbonding.ca` as part of the acquisition (and the Assignor consents in writing instead of exercising Option A), the Assignor shall be paid a dedicated closing buyout payout equal to **[2.5x – 3.0x]** the trailing 12 months' gross annual commission and net referral revenue generated by leads tagged `CB-WEB` (`contractbonding.ca`), paid directly at closing before net sale proceeds are distributed to the Brokerage's shareholders.

5.5 **Survival:** Section 5 survives termination of the Producer's employment/engagement and is binding upon the Brokerage's successors and assigns.
