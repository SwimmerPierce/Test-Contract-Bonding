# Note on Option 2 Fallback (Corporate Assignment + $1.00 Reversion)

## Why Option 1 Was Selected for This Execution Package
The main contract in this package (`01_Domain_and_Platform_License_Addendum_(Execution_Ready).docx`) uses the **Option 1 Licensing Structure** (Chase retains personal title to `contractbonding.ca` and the codebase, and grants Nexus an exclusive operating license) merged with Option 2's governance, $2,000/month corporate funding, and 12-month installment protections.

Option 1 avoids transferring domain/IP title into the corporation and then pulling it back out for $1.00 upon termination, which minimizes tax, creditor, and minority-shareholder friction.

---

## When Option 2 Would Be Needed
The original Option 2 draft (available in `05_Reference_Original_Drafts_and_Comparison.docx`) should only be used if one of the following third parties explicitly insists that **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. must hold legal title to `contractbonding.ca` during active operations**:

1. **Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurer:** If Nexus's E&O carrier refuses to endorse `contractbonding.ca` under an exclusive operating license and requires corporate domain ownership as a condition of coverage.
2. **Google Ads / Merchant Verification:** If Google Ads business verification or financial services advertiser verification requires the CIRA domain registrant to match Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.'s corporate entity.
3. **Insurance Council / DBA Registration:** If a provincial regulator or corporate lawyer advises that corporate title is required for trade name/DBA compliance or audit comfort.
