### 1. Purpose & Licensing Warranties
1.1 **Valid Licensing:** Each Party warrants that it holds all active corporate and individual insurance brokerage licences required in its respective province(s) of operation. Referring Brokerage is licensed in BC and Alberta and does **not** solicit, advise on, negotiate, or bind insurance/surety contracts in Receiving Brokerage's province.  
1.2 **Scope of Referrals:** From time to time, Referring Brokerage may refer prospective commercial or construction surety bond clients located in **[TARGET PROVINCE]** (each a **"Referred Prospect"**) who inquire via `contractbonding.ca` or directly with Referring Brokerage.

### 2. Strictly Non-Solicitation Referral Role
2.1 Referring Brokerage's role is strictly limited to introducing the Referred Prospect and transmitting basic contact/inquiry information after obtaining the Referred Prospect's consent.  
2.2 Receiving Brokerage assumes 100% responsibility for licensing compliance, client underwriting, needs assessment, surety placement, policy/bond execution, statutory disclosures, and ongoing servicing in **[TARGET PROVINCE]**.

### 3. Referral Compensation & Payment Mechanics
3.1 **Permitted Licensee-to-Licensee Payment:** In accordance with applicable provincial insurance statutes (including BC FIA s. 178(2), Alberta *Insurance Act* s. 499, and RIBO O. Reg. 991, s. 15 permitting referrals between licensed insurance brokers across Canadian jurisdictions), Receiving Brokerage agrees to pay Referring Brokerage (**Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.**) the following referral fee for each Referred Prospect that binds a surety bond or commercial insurance policy through Receiving Brokerage:
- **[Choose one structure per jurisdiction — note RIBO guidance below]:**
  - **Structure A (Percentage Referral / Co-Brokerage Fee — Where Permitted by Target Regulator):** **[20% – 30%]** of gross earned agency commissions on Year 1 surety bond and commercial policy placements (and **[10% – 20%]** on renewals while Receiving Brokerage services the account), payable within 30 days of Receiving Brokerage receiving cleared insurer commissions.  
  - **Structure B (Fixed One-Time Referral Fee — Conservative RIBO Option):** A flat, one-time referral fee of **$[AMOUNT] CAD** per closed bond facility/account referred *(recommended if Receiving Brokerage's RIBO compliance officer interprets O. Reg. 991 as requiring non-recurring flat referral fees when the referring out-of-province broker does not hold a RIBO licence)*.
3.2 **Payee Requirement:** All payments must be made strictly payable to **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** (never to an individual salesperson or unlicensed marketing entity).

### 4. Mandatory Client Disclosure & Privacy Compliance
4.1 Both Parties agree that before or at the time of referral handoff, the Referred Prospect must receive written notice disclosing:  
   - (a) That Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. is not licensed in **[TARGET PROVINCE]** and is referring the inquiry to **[PARTNER BROKERAGE LEGAL NAME]**;  
   - (b) That Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. will receive a referral fee/compensation from Receiving Brokerage if a bond or policy is placed; and  
   - (c) That the Referred Prospect is under no obligation to proceed with Receiving Brokerage.  
4.2 Each Party shall comply with BC PIPA, Alberta PIPA, PIPEDA, and CASL with respect to Referred Prospect data.

### 5. Non-Circumvention & Term
5.1 Receiving Brokerage shall not circumvent Referring Brokerage to avoid referral compensation on Referred Prospects introduced in writing under this Agreement for a period of **24 months** following written introduction.  
5.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on **30 days' written notice**; termination does not extinguish earned referral fees on Referred Prospects already bound or introduced prior to termination.
