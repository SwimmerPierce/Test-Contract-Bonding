## 1. Purpose
While the Insurance Council of British Columbia removed the legacy physical-premises restriction from Rule 6(1)(b)—permitting Level 1 General Insurance Salespersons to work remotely or through online portals—**Council Rule 6(1)(c)** mandates that a Level 1 salesperson may conduct insurance business **only under the direct supervision of a general insurance agent (Level 2 or Level 3)**. This written protocol documents the mandatory supervision, underwriting review, and contract-signing controls governing `contractbonding.ca`.

---

## 2. Hard Statutory Prohibitions (`Rule 6(1)(a)`)
1. **Zero Bond or Policy Signing Authority:** Under **Council Rule 6(1)(a)**, the Level 1 salesperson **must never sign, seal, countersign, or issue** any contract of insurance or surety bond on behalf of an insurer or surety company—including:
   - Bid Bonds & Consents of Surety (Agreements to Bond)
   - Performance Bonds & Labour and Material Payment Bonds
   - Maintenance, Developer/Subdivision, TSBC Electrical/Gas, or Alberta Prepaid Contractor Bonds
   - Commercial insurance binders, certificates of insurance, or interim receipts
2. **No Signature Stamps or Automated Signing:** The Level 1 salesperson shall never use a physical or electronic signature stamp or Power of Attorney credential belonging to a Level 2 or Level 3 agent (`Rule 6(1)(a)` guidance).
3. **Mandatory Level 2/3 Execution:** Every bond, Consent of Surety, or insurance binder generated from `contractbonding.ca` must be reviewed, approved, and executed by a licensed **Level 2 or Level 3 General Insurance Agent** at Nexus holding valid surety Power of Attorney.

---

## 3. Scope of Permitted Duties for Chase (Level 1 / CAIB 1)
Subject to direct supervision by the Level 3 Nominee or a designated Level 2/3 supervisor, the Level 1 salesperson may:
- Maintain the `contractbonding.ca` website content and non-binding marketing materials (with Nominee approval);
- Conduct initial intake calls with BC leads (and Alberta leads **only after** receiving an active individual AIC Non-Resident Level 1 Certificate of Authority);
- Gather contractor questionnaires, financial statements, WIP schedules, and project/tender details;
- Coordinate Commercial General Liability (CGL), Builders Risk, Tools, and Commercial Auto quotes under standard Level 1 commercial supervision; and
- Route complex surety underwriting files (Stream B) to senior Nexus brokers and out-of-province inquiries (Stream C) to pre-approved partner brokerages.

---

## 4. Digital & Remote Supervision Controls (`Rule 6(1)(c)`)
1. **Shared `/admin` Dashboard Visibility:** The Supervising Level 2/3 Agent holds direct login access to `https://contractbonding.ca/admin` and receives real-time email notifications on every incoming lead from `POST /api/leads`.
2. **48-Hour Urgent Tender Escalation:** Any Bid Bond or Consent of Surety lead with a `Required By Date` within 5 business days must be flagged immediately to the Supervising Level 2/3 Agent (or handled via direct phone handoff at `604-420-2501`) to prevent missed tender deadlines and E&O claims.
3. **Weekly File & Communication Audit:** At least once per week, the Supervising Agent reviews open leads in `/admin` and Nexus's Broker Management System (BMS), verifying:
   - Proper provincial licensing compliance (no unlicensed Alberta solicitation prior to AIC licence issuance; proper out-of-province referral disclosures);
   - Accurate underwriting notes and client disclosures (`B.C. Reg. 573/2004`);
   - Confirmation that a Level 2/3 agent signed and delivered all final bonds and binders.
4. **Provincial Restriction Check:** All leads outside **BC** and **Alberta** are strictly barred from quoting/solicitation by Nexus staff and may only be referred under Nexus's signed Interprovincial Broker-to-Broker Referral Agreements after verifying client referral consent.

---

## 5. Privacy & Security Safeguards (`BC/AB PIPA s. 34`)
- Neither the Level 1 salesperson nor any staff member may export lead CSVs to unsecured personal devices or unencrypted third-party spreadsheets.
- All financial statements, personal net worth forms, and tender documents received after intake must be stored directly inside Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.'s encrypted BMS/document management system, not inside the public web application's `surety_leads` table.
