## Template C: Alberta Insurance Council (AIC) — Trade Name & Individual Non-Resident Level 1 Guide
*(Governed by Alberta *Insurance Act* s. 452 & s. 490 and AIC non-resident administrative rules)*

### 1. Agency Trade Name Alignment (If Registering a DBA in BC)
Under AIC administrative rules, a non-resident agency must apply/operate in Alberta under the **exact same corporate and trade name** licensed in its home jurisdiction (BC):
- [ ] Register the exact matching trade name with **Alberta Corporate Registries** (obtain Alberta Corporate Access Number / Extra-Provincial Trade Name registration).
- [ ] Confirm Nexus's E&O policy certificate explicitly reflects both `Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.` and the registered trade name.
- [ ] Submit the Trade Name update to the **Alberta Insurance Council** (`abcouncil.ab.ca`).

### 2. Chase's Individual Non-Resident General Level 1 Application (No Exam Required)
Because corporate licensing does **not** permit an individual salesperson without an Alberta certificate to transact Alberta risks (`Alberta Insurance Act s. 452`), complete these steps via `abcouncil.ab.ca`:
- [ ] **Step 1:** Confirm your **CIPR Number** and active **BC General Insurance Level 1 Salesperson** status (must be active within the past 12 months for exam-exempt BC–AB reciprocity).
- [ ] **Step 2:** Obtain a current **Criminal Record Check** meeting AIC submission recency requirements.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Submit the **Non-Resident General Insurance Level 1 Agent** application in the AIC Portal, selecting **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** as your sponsoring agency.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Have your father (Nexus's Alberta Designated Representative) log into the AIC portal to **approve sponsorship** and confirm E&O coverage (`abcouncil.ab.ca`).
- [ ] **Step 5:** Pay the AIC licensing fee and download your Alberta Certificate of Authority before contacting or quoting any Alberta surety lead directly.
