- (b) All bonds and contracts must be reviewed and executed solely by a licensed Level 2 or Level 3 General Insurance Agent of the Brokerage holding valid Attorney-in-Fact authority; and
- (c) All insurance and surety activities conducted by the Producer via the Platform shall be carried out under the direct supervision of the Brokerage's Level 3 Nominee or designated Level 2/3 supervisor (`Rule 6(1)(c)`), who shall be provided ongoing administrative access to the Platform's `/admin` lead dashboard for supervisory review and auditing.

4.2 **Level 2 Licence Upgrade & Expanded Surety Bonding Capacity:** The Parties expressly agree that the Producer's operational role and bonding capacity are intended to scale as the Producer advances in licensing:

- (a) **Removal of Level 1 Supervision & Signing Restrictions:** Immediately upon the Producer obtaining a **Level 2 General Insurance Agent** licence (or higher, such as CAIB 2–3 / Level 2 or CAIB 4 / Level 3) in good standing, the Level 1 supervision and bond-signing restrictions in Section 4.1 automatically cease to apply to the extent permitted by law;
- (b) **Expanded Bonding Authority & Surety Capacity:** Upon obtaining a Level 2 licence (or higher), the Brokerage shall grant the Producer expanded operational, underwriting, and account-servicing authority to originate, underwrite, manage, and retain complex surety bond facilities directly (rather than handing them off internally), subject only to standard brokerage underwriting guidelines and insurer market limits;
- (c) **Attorney-in-Fact (Power of Attorney) Appointments:** Upon the Producer achieving Level 2 licensing, the Brokerage agrees to actively support, sponsor, and nominate the Producer for **Attorney-in-Fact / Power of Attorney** bond-signing appointments with the Brokerage's contracted surety insurers (as eligible under each insurer's underwriting standards) so the Producer can sign, seal, and issue bonds and Consents of Surety directly; and

4.3 **Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance (`Insurance Council Rule 7(11)`):** The Brokerage shall notify its Errors & Omissions insurer of the operation of `contractbonding.ca` (and any registered DBA) as a service of the Brokerage and maintain active E&O coverage meeting or exceeding statutory minimums (`$1,000,000` per claim / `$2,000,000` aggregate) covering all licensed activities conducted through the Platform.

## 5. Operational Jurisdictions & Asymmetric Provincial Expansion

5.1 **Definitions of Operational Jurisdiction:**

- (a) **"Authorized Brokerage Jurisdictions"** means British Columbia and Alberta as of the Effective Date, **plus any additional Canadian province or territory** in which the Brokerage subsequently obtains a corporate or non-resident insurance agency licence over time.
- (b) **"Producer Licensed Jurisdictions"** means British Columbia as of the Effective Date, **plus any additional Canadian province or territory** in which the Producer subsequently obtains an individual resident or non-resident insurance licence (including, without limitation, Alberta via interprovincial reciprocity or other provinces as the Producer expands).

5.2 **Recognition of Different Expansion Timelines:** The Parties acknowledge that the Brokerage and the Producer may expand their respective provincial licences at **different times and rates**. Specifically, the Brokerage may gain corporate authority to transact in a new province before the Producer obtains an individual licence in that province (or vice versa, where an individual non-resident licence awaits corporate agency sponsorship). Lead routing, authority to work on a deal, and commission categorization on every `contractbonding.ca` lead are governed dynamically by the three tiers below at the time the business is written or renewed:

- **Tier 1 — Both Brokerage & Producer Licensed in the Province (`Stream A`):** Where both the Brokerage holds an agency licence and the Producer holds an active individual licence in the client's/project's province (and the Producer handles the deal within their licence level), the Producer may actively solicit, underwrite (within Level 1/Level 2 authority), and service the account directly under **Stream A** (**65% Year 1 / 35% Renewal**).
- **Tier 2 — Brokerage Licensed, Producer Not Yet Licensed in the Province (`Stream B`):** Where the Brokerage is licensed in the province (e.g., Alberta prior to the Producer's individual Alberta certificate, or any future province the Brokerage enters before the Producer is individually licensed there), the Producer shall **not** personally solicit, quote, or advise on the risk. The lead shall be immediately routed internally to an appropriately licensed Nexus broker under **Stream B** (**35% Year 1**). If the Producer subsequently obtains an individual licence in that province and actively takes over working on the account by renewal, the renewal split automatically upgrades from **15%** to **35%** per Schedule A.
- **Tier 3 — Brokerage Not Yet Licensed in the Province (`Stream C`):** Where the Brokerage does not hold an agency licence in the client's/project's province, the lead shall be referred by **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** to an appropriately licensed external partner brokerage under **Stream C** (**90% of the referral fee** to Producer). If the Brokerage subsequently obtains a corporate/non-resident agency licence in that province, new leads originating in that province thereafter automatically transition in-house under **Tier 1 (Stream A)** or **Tier 2 (Stream B)** based on whether the Producer is individually licensed there.
