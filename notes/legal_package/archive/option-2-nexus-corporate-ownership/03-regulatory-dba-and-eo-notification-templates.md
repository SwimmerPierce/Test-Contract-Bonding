## Template C: Alberta Insurance Council (AIC) Non-Resident DBA & Individual Level 1 Guide
*(Governed by Alberta *Insurance Act* s. 452 & s. 490)*

### 1. Corporate Non-Resident DBA Alignment
1. **Register Matching DBA in Alberta:** File the exact same DBA name registered in BC (`[Contract Bonding / contractbonding.ca]`) with **Alberta Corporate Registries** (obtain Alberta Corporate Access confirmation).
2. **Confirm E&O Policy Match:** AIC rules require the non-resident agency's E&O policy to explicitly list both the corporate legal name and any DBA used in Alberta.
3. **Update AIC Non-Resident Agency Licence:** Upload the Alberta trade name confirmation and updated E&O certificate to `abcouncil.ab.ca`.
4. **Website Display (`AB Insurance Act s. 490`):** Conspicuously display `Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.` (and the registered trade name) across every web page, calculator, modal, and ad.

### 2. Chase's Individual Non-Resident Level 1 Application (BC–AB Reciprocity)
- [ ] Apply on `abcouncil.ab.ca` using your CIPR number under **BC–AB Equivalency (no exam required)**.
- [ ] Attach valid BC Level 1 licence status, current Criminal Record Check, and E&O coverage confirmation.
- [ ] Have Father approve sponsorship in the AIC portal as Designated Representative.
- [ ] **Interim Rule:** Until your AIC individual certificate is approved, transfer all Alberta leads at intake to an AIC-licensed Nexus broker.
