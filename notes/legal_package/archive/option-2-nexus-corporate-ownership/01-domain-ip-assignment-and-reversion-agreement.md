## 4. Operational Jurisdictions & Asymmetric Provincial Expansion

4.1 **Definitions of Operational Jurisdiction:**

- (a) **"Authorized Brokerage Jurisdictions"** means British Columbia and Alberta as of the Effective Date, **plus any additional Canadian province or territory** in which the Brokerage subsequently obtains a corporate or non-resident insurance agency licence over time.
- (b) **"Producer Licensed Jurisdictions"** means British Columbia as of the Effective Date, **plus any additional Canadian province or territory** in which the Producer subsequently obtains an individual resident or non-resident insurance licence (including Alberta via interprovincial reciprocity or other provinces over time).

4.2 **Recognition of Different Expansion Timelines:** The Parties acknowledge that the Brokerage and the Producer may gain authority to transact in additional provinces at **different times and rates**. Lead routing, servicing rights, and commission categorization on every `contractbonding.ca` lead are governed dynamically by the three tiers below at the time the business is written or renewed:

- **Tier 1 — Both Brokerage & Producer Licensed in the Province (`Stream A`):** Where both the Brokerage holds an agency licence and the Producer holds an active individual licence in the client's/project's province (and the Producer works on the deal within their licence level), the Producer works on and services the account directly under **Stream A** (**65% Year 1 / 35% Renewal**).
- **Tier 2 — Brokerage Licensed, Producer Not Yet Licensed in the Province (`Stream B`):** Where the Brokerage is licensed in the province (e.g., Alberta prior to the Producer's individual Alberta certificate, or any future province the Brokerage enters before the Producer is individually licensed there), the Producer shall **not** personally solicit, quote, or advise on the risk. The lead is immediately routed internally to an appropriately licensed Nexus broker under **Stream B** (**35% Year 1**). If the Producer subsequently becomes individually licensed in that province and actively works on the account by renewal, the renewal split upgrades from **15%** to **35%** per Schedule A.
- **Tier 3 — Brokerage Not Yet Licensed in the Province (`Stream C`):** Where the Brokerage does not hold an agency licence in the client's/project's province, the lead is referred by **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** to an appropriately licensed external partner brokerage under **Stream C** (**90% of the referral fee** to Producer). If the Brokerage subsequently obtains a corporate/non-resident agency licence in that province, subsequent new leads in that province automatically move in-house under **Tier 1 (Stream A)** or **Tier 2 (Stream B)** depending on whether the Producer holds an individual licence there.

## 5. Mandatory Asset Reversion, Departure Protections & Book Buyout (`4.0x – 4.5x`)

*Because the Assignor built the Platform Assets and is assigning legal title solely to streamline regulatory licensing and pre-tax corporate marketing deductions, the Parties expressly agree that the following protections are a fundamental condition of the assignment:*

5.1 **Restriction on Third-Party Transfer:** The Brokerage shall **not** sell, assign, pledge, license, or transfer the Platform Assets (`contractbonding.ca`, its web hosting infrastructure, database hosting structure, or source code repository) to any unrelated third party, nor allow any third-party lien to attach thereto, without the Assignor's prior written consent.

5.2 **Trigger Events (All Departure & M&A Scenarios Covered):** A **"Trigger Event"** occurs if any of the following takes place:

- (a) **Change of Control / M&A Sale:** The Brokerage enters into an agreement to sell all or substantially all of its assets, book of business, or controlling shares to an unrelated third party;
- (b) **Termination Without Just Cause:** The Assignor's employment or producer engagement with the Brokerage is terminated without just cause;
- (c) **Voluntary Resignation / Departure by Assignor:** The Assignor chooses to leave the Brokerage upon **30 days' written notice** (or transfers the Assignor's insurance licence to another brokerage);
- (d) **Termination With Just Cause:** The Assignor's employment or producer engagement with the Brokerage is terminated with just cause; or
- (e) **Wind-Down, Loss of Licence, or Payment Default:** The Brokerage ceases active operations, winds down, loses its general insurance agency licence, or defaults on Schedule A commission payments for more than 45 days after written notice.

5.3 **Dual Protection — Take Back Domain & Code (`$1.00`) PLUS `[4.0x – 4.5x]` Deal / Book-of-Business Buyout Payout:**

*The Parties expressly distinguish between (i) the Platform technology (`contractbonding.ca` domain and Web Application code), (ii) Excluded Personal IP (`Schedule B`, which always remains outside this Agreement), and (iii) the bound deals and recurring client book tagged **`CB-WEB`** (as defined in Section 2.2 to include both inbound `contractbonding.ca` leads and outbound deals originated via Excluded Personal IP exports such as `BC_Bid_Organizer_Skill`), which remain on the Brokerage's books or pass to an M&A acquirer.*

Upon **any** Trigger Event under Section 5.2(a)–(e), the Assignor is entitled to exercise **BOTH** of the following rights simultaneously:

- (a) **Take Back 100% Ownership & Control of Domain and Code (`$1.00 CAD`):** **100% legal and beneficial title to `contractbonding.ca` and the Web Application source code repository (`SwimmerPierce/Test-Contract-Bonding`, including DNS records, web hosting infrastructure, and database structure, excluding confidential client policy underwriting files required by law to remain with the Brokerage) shall automatically revert and be re-assigned to Chase Addison for $1.00 CAD.** Within **10 business days** of written notice from the Assignor, the Brokerage shall execute all CIRA registrant transfer authorizations and repository/infrastructure transfers required to return full control to the Assignor (without ever affecting Excluded Personal IP under Schedule B, which never left Chase Addison's ownership); **AND**
- (b) **Dedicated `[4.0x – 4.5x]` Deal / Book-of-Business Buyout Payout (With M&A Upside Ratchet):** In consideration for the Brokerage (or an M&A buyer) retaining the bound **`CB-WEB`** deals and recurring client book, the Assignor shall be paid a dedicated buyout payout (plus any unpaid earned commissions accrued prior to the Trigger Event) calculated as follows:
  - *(i) Upon Internal Departure or Non-Sale Trigger (`§5.2(b)–(e)`):* **`[4.0x – 4.5x]`** the trailing 12 months' gross annual commission and net referral revenue generated by the entire **`CB-WEB`** book (as defined in Section 2.2), payable within **30 days** (or via an agreed installment schedule secured against the `CB-WEB` commission stream); **OR**
  - *(ii) Upon Third-Party Sale / Change of Control (`§5.2(a)` — M&A Upside Ratchet):* **The greater of** (A) **`[4.0x – 4.5x]`** the trailing 12 months' gross annual commission and net referral revenue of the `CB-WEB` book, **or** (B) **`75%` of the actual headline gross commission-revenue multiple** paid by the third-party buyer for Nexus's book applied to the `CB-WEB` trailing 12-month revenue *(for example, at a `6.0x` revenue exit, `75%` = `4.5x`; if Nexus exits at `6.5x`, Assignor receives `4.875x`)*, payable directly at closing before net sale proceeds are distributed to the Brokerage's shareholders.  
  *(Optional Transfer Alternative: Upon departure under Section 5.2(b) or (c) to another licensed brokerage, the Assignor may alternatively elect in writing—subject to client consent and insurer appointments—to transfer the `CB-WEB` client book servicing rights in lieu of the cash buyout payout.)*

5.4 **Separate & Non-Offsetting Payout (And Optional M&A IP Retention):**

- (a) **Separate & Non-Offsetting Payout:** For certainty, the `[4.0x – 4.5x]` (or M&A Ratchet) buyout payout under Section 5.3(b) is completely **separate from, independent of, and in addition to** (and shall not be credited against, offset, or reduce) any other payout, share or equity sale proceeds, family succession/gifting distribution, retention bonus, earn-out, severance, or earned producer commissions that the Assignor may otherwise receive in connection with an M&A buyout or departure.

The commission percentages set out in **Schedule A** above represent a **guaranteed contractual minimum floor** for the Producer on all **`CB-WEB`** business (covering both inbound leads generated through `contractbonding.ca` and outbound deals originated via Schedule B tools such as `BC_Bid_Organizer_Skill`):
