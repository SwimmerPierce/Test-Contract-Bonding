## 1. Recitals & Definitions
1.1 **Ownership of Domain & Platform:** The Producer is the sole personal registrant of the Canadian Internet Registration Authority (CIRA) domain name **`contractbonding.ca`** and the sole creator and owner of the associated website, source code repository, calculator logic, digital assets, and administrative software web application (collectively, the **"Platform"**).  
1.2 **Current Producer Licensing:** As of the Effective Date, the Producer holds a General Insurance Level 1 Salesperson licence (CAIB 1) in British Columbia sponsored by and operating under the supervision of the Brokerage and its Level 3 Nominee / Designated Representative.  
1.3 **Regulatory Compliance Objective:** Under the British Columbia *Financial Institutions Act* (RSBC 1996, c. 141), the Insurance Council of BC Rules (including Rules 6 and 7), the Alberta *Insurance Act* (RSA 2000, c. I-3), and equivalent provincial insurance statutes, all insurance and surety bond solicitation, underwriting submissions, commission collection, interprovincial referrals, and personal information control must be conducted solely by and through an appropriately licensed insurance agency and individually licensed intermediaries.  
1.4 **Intent of Parties:** The Parties wish to operate the Platform publicly as an official surety service of the Brokerage while preserving the Producer's personal title to the Platform, establishing clear rules for licence progression and multi-province expansion, and defining producer compensation across all revenue streams.

## 4. Regulatory Supervision, Level 2 Upgrade & Expanded Bonding Capacity
4.1 **Level 1 Authority Limits (`Insurance Council Rule 6(1)(a)` & `Rule 6(1)(c)`):** For so long as the Producer holds a **Level 1 Salesperson** licence:  
   - (a) The Producer shall **not** sign, seal, or execute any surety bond (including Bid Bonds, Consents of Surety, Performance Bonds, Labour & Material Payment Bonds, or License/Administrative Bonds), insurance binder, certificate, or contract on behalf of an insurer, nor use any signature stamp of a Level 2/3 agent (`Rule 6(1)(a)`);  
   - (b) All bonds and contracts must be reviewed and executed solely by a licensed Level 2 or Level 3 General Insurance Agent of the Brokerage holding valid Attorney-in-Fact authority; and  
   - (c) All insurance and surety activities conducted by the Producer via the Platform shall be carried out under the direct supervision of the Brokerage's Level 3 Nominee or designated Level 2/3 supervisor (`Rule 6(1)(c)`), who shall be provided ongoing administrative access to the Platform's `/admin` lead dashboard for supervisory review and auditing.

4.2 **Level 2 Licence Upgrade & Expanded Surety Bonding Capacity:** The Parties expressly agree that the Producer's operational role and bonding capacity are intended to scale as the Producer advances in licensing:  
   - (a) **Removal of Level 1 Supervision & Signing Restrictions:** Immediately upon the Producer obtaining a **Level 2 General Insurance Agent** licence (or higher, such as CAIB 2–3 / Level 2 or CAIB 4 / Level 3) in good standing, the Level 1 supervision and bond-signing restrictions in Section 4.1 automatically cease to apply to the extent permitted by law;  
   - (b) **Expanded Bonding Authority & Surety Capacity:** Upon obtaining a Level 2 licence (or higher), the Brokerage shall grant the Producer expanded operational, underwriting, and account-servicing authority to originate, underwrite, manage, and retain complex surety bond facilities directly (rather than handing them off internally), subject only to standard brokerage underwriting guidelines and insurer market limits;  
   - (c) **Attorney-in-Fact (Power of Attorney) Appointments:** Upon the Producer achieving Level 2 licensing, the Brokerage agrees to actively support, sponsor, and nominate the Producer for **Attorney-in-Fact / Power of Attorney** bond-signing appointments with the Brokerage's contracted surety insurers (as eligible under each insurer's underwriting standards) so the Producer can sign, seal, and issue bonds and Consents of Surety directly; and  
   - (d) **Right to Assume Servicing on Renewals:** Upon upgrading to Level 2 (or obtaining any required provincial licence), the Producer may assume active ongoing servicing of previously handed-off Stream B accounts at renewal, thereby upgrading the Producer's renewal compensation on those accounts from the non-servicing rate (**15%**) to the active-servicing rate (**35%**) as set out in Schedule A.

4.3 **Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance (`Insurance Council Rule 7(11)`):** The Brokerage shall notify its Errors & Omissions insurer of the operation of `contractbonding.ca` (and any registered DBA) as a service of the Brokerage and maintain active E&O coverage meeting or exceeding statutory minimums (`$1,000,000` per claim / `$2,000,000` aggregate) covering all licensed activities conducted through the Platform.  
4.4 **Privacy & CASL Control:** The Brokerage acts as the accountable organization under BC PIPA, Alberta PIPA (including Section 13.1), and CASL for personal information collected on the Platform. Both Parties agree to maintain required privacy notices, CASL consent logs, and security safeguards.

## 5. Operational Jurisdictions & Asymmetric Provincial Expansion
5.1 **Definitions of Operational Jurisdiction:**  
   - (a) **"Authorized Brokerage Jurisdictions"** means British Columbia and Alberta as of the Effective Date, **plus any additional Canadian province or territory** in which the Brokerage subsequently obtains a corporate or non-resident insurance agency licence over time.  
   - (b) **"Producer Licensed Jurisdictions"** means British Columbia as of the Effective Date, **plus any additional Canadian province or territory** in which the Producer subsequently obtains an individual resident or non-resident insurance licence (including, without limitation, Alberta via interprovincial reciprocity or other provinces as the Producer expands).

5.2 **Recognition of Different Expansion Timelines:** The Parties acknowledge that the Brokerage and the Producer may expand their respective provincial licences at **different times and rates**. Specifically, the Brokerage may gain corporate authority to transact in a new province before the Producer obtains an individual licence in that province (or vice versa, where an individual non-resident licence awaits corporate agency sponsorship). Lead routing, authority to work on a deal, and commission categorization on every `contractbonding.ca` lead are governed dynamically by the three tiers below at the time the business is written or renewed:

   - **Tier 1 — Both Brokerage & Producer Licensed in the Province (`Stream A`):** Where both the Brokerage holds an agency licence and the Producer holds an active individual licence in the client's/project's province (and the Producer handles the deal within their licence level), the Producer may actively solicit, underwrite (within Level 1/Level 2 authority), and service the account directly under **Stream A** (**65% Year 1 / 35% Renewal**).
   - **Tier 2 — Brokerage Licensed, Producer Not Yet Licensed in the Province (`Stream B`):** Where the Brokerage is licensed in the province (e.g., Alberta prior to the Producer's individual Alberta certificate, or any future province the Brokerage enters before the Producer is individually licensed there), the Producer shall **not** personally solicit, quote, or advise on the risk. The lead shall be immediately routed internally to an appropriately licensed Nexus broker under **Stream B** (**35% Year 1**). If the Producer subsequently obtains an individual licence in that province and actively takes over working on the account by renewal, the renewal split automatically upgrades from **15%** to **35%** per Schedule A.
   - **Tier 3 — Brokerage Not Yet Licensed in the Province (`Stream C`):** Where the Brokerage does not hold an agency licence in the client's/project's province, the lead shall be referred by **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** to an appropriately licensed external partner brokerage under **Stream C** (**90% of the referral fee** to Producer). If the Brokerage subsequently obtains a corporate/non-resident agency licence in that province, new leads originating in that province thereafter automatically transition in-house under **Tier 1 (Stream A)** or **Tier 2 (Stream B)** based on whether the Producer is individually licensed there.

## 7. Term, Termination & Sale of Brokerage Protection
7.1 **Term:** This Addendum commences on the Effective Date and continues for so long as the Producer remains actively licensed with and sponsored by the Brokerage, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Section.  
7.2 **Termination:** Either Party may terminate this Addendum upon **30 days' written notice** (or immediately if required by the Insurance Council of BC or any applicable provincial regulator).  
7.3 **Sale or Change of Control of Brokerage:** If Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. enters into an agreement to sell its shares or book of business to an unrelated third party, the License granted under Section 2 shall **not** automatically transfer to the buyer. Upon closing of any third-party sale without a new written license or buyout agreement signed by the Producer:  
   - (a) The License terminates automatically upon closing;  
   - (b) The Producer retains sole authority to redirect `contractbonding.ca`, sell/license the Platform separately, or negotiate a separate Platform purchase/licensing fee with the acquirer; and  
   - (c) Any earned and unpaid commissions or renewal splits accrued prior to termination remain payable to the Producer per Schedule A.

## Schedule A — Three-Tier Producer Commission & Referral Split Structure

All leads tagged to `contractbonding.ca` (and related outbound campaigns originated by the Producer under the `contractbonding.ca` brand) are categorized into one of three revenue streams paid to the Producer through the Brokerage's standard commission payroll:

| Revenue Stream | Description of Business | Producer Commission Share | Brokerage Share | Notes & Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stream A: Producer-Worked Business** | Surety bonds and cross-sold Commercial Insurance (CGL, Builders Risk, Tools, Commercial Auto) originated and actively worked on by the Producer in a jurisdiction where both the Brokerage and Producer are licensed (under Level 1 supervision initially, and with expanded bonding capacity upon Level 2 licensing) | **Year 1 (First Year):** **65%** of gross brokerage commission<br>**Year 2 & Subsequent Renewals:** **35%** of gross brokerage commission | **Year 1:** **35%**<br>**Year 2 & Renewals:** **65%** | While Level 1, a Level 2/3 agent signs/executes all surety bonds (`Rule 6(1)(a)`). Upon achieving Level 2 and Power of Attorney appointments, Producer may execute eligible bonds directly. |
| **Stream B: Complex Handoff or Brokerage-Only Jurisdiction** | Complex surety accounts outside Producer's current capacity/authority, or leads in an Authorized Brokerage Jurisdiction where the Producer is not yet individually licensed, handed off internally to another Nexus broker | **Year 1 (First Year):** **35%** of gross brokerage commission<br>**Renewals (Producer NOT working on deal):** **15%** of gross commission<br>**Renewals (Producer IS working on deal by renewal):** **35%** of gross commission | Balance split between Servicing Broker & Brokerage house | Applies to both surety bond commissions **and** cross-sold CGL/Commercial policies written on that referred client. Upgrading to Level 2 or obtaining the target provincial licence enables Producer to work the deal at renewal and earn the **35%** active renewal split. |
| **Stream C: Out-of-Province External Referrals** | Leads in provinces/territories where the Brokerage is not yet licensed, referred by **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** under a written referral agreement to an external licensed partner brokerage | **90%** of the referral fee received by Nexus | **10%** administrative share | All external referral agreements and fee checks/EFTs are contracted and collected strictly in the name of **Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.** |
