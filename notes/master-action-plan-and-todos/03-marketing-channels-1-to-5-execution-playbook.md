## Channel 1: Automated Public Procurement Outbound Engine (BC Bid, APC & Licensing Portals)

### Sub-Tasks Checklist
- [ ] **1.1 Weekly Procurement Data Pull:** Every Monday at 9:00 AM, query **BC Bid (`bcbid.gov.bc.ca`)** (`bc-contract-awards` & `bc-unverified-bids`), **Alberta Purchasing Connection (`purchasingconnection.ca`)**, and municipal award logs (Burnaby, Vancouver, Surrey, Metro Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton).
- [ ] **1.2 Segment Prospects Into 3 Buckets:**
  - **Bucket A (`$75,000 – $250,000` Unbonded Award Winners):** Growing trades/GCs winning small municipal jobs without bonding who want to bid `$500K–$2M` public tenders next year.
  - **Bucket B (2nd, 3rd & 4th Place Bidders on Bonded Tenders):** Contractors appearing in `Unverified Bid Results` who already bid regularly and rely on timely Bid Bonds/Consents of Surety.
  - **Bucket C (Land Developers & Civil Firms Pulling Building/Subdivision Permits):** Developers posting 100% cash/bank Letters of Credit (LOCs) for municipal servicing works.
- [ ] **1.3 Technical Safety BC & Service Alberta Registry Prospecting:** Track newly registered electrical/gas contractors in BC (`$10,000` TSBC bond) and residential renos/roofing/HVAC contractors in Alberta (`$10,000` Prepaid Contractor bond).
- [ ] **1.4 CASL-Safe Outreach Discipline:** Prefer **phone calls, LinkedIn messages, or physical mail** for cold outbound (exempt from CASL email consent restrictions). When emailing a business email conspicuously published on a corporate site (`CASL s. 10(9)(b)`), ensure the message is directly relevant to their construction/bidding role and includes Nexus's Burnaby address, phone, and an unsubscribe line.
