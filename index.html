Surety Fundamentals

What is Bonding in Construction?

A construction surety bond is a legally binding, three-party financial guarantee that protects project owners and public taxpayers by ensuring a contractor fulfills its contractual obligations.

01

The Principal (Contractor)

The general contractor, subcontractor, or business purchasing the bond and committing to complete the project or obligation according to contract terms.

02

The Obligee (Project Owner)

The municipality, government agency, developer, or general contractor requiring the bond as financial security against default or non-performance.

03

The Surety (Bonding Company)

The licensed surety insurer—arranged through Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.—that pre-qualifies the contractor and backs the guarantee financially.

Risk Mitigation

Why are Construction Bonds Needed?

Public tenders, municipal infrastructure projects, and major private developments in British Columbia routinely require surety bonds before a contractor can even submit a bid. Bonds filter out unqualified bidders and provide financial continuity if a contractor defaults mid-project.

Unlike traditional insurance, surety underwriting evaluates your company’s track record and financial strength using the 3 C’s of Surety:

C

Character

Reputation, industry references, dispute history, and proven integrity.

C

Capacity

Technical expertise, equipment, key personnel, and experience on similar scopes.

C

Capital

Working capital, net worth, credit lines, and accountant-prepared financials.

What Types of Construction Bonds are Available?

From tender day through warranty closeout and commercial licensing, we structure the exact bond your contract or authority requires.

Tender Bonds

Pre-Award Stage
  • Bid Bond Guarantees the bidder will enter into the contract at the submitted price (typically 10% of bid).
  • Consent of Surety (Agreement to Bond) Confirms the surety’s commitment to issue final Performance and L&M Payment bonds upon award.

Final Bonds

Contract Execution
  • Performance Bond Guarantees project completion according to specifications, drawings, and schedule (50%–100%).
  • Labour & Material Payment Bond Ensures subcontractors and material suppliers are paid if the contractor defaults.
  • Maintenance Bond Covers workmanship and material defects during the post-completion warranty period.

Other & Administrative Bonds

Licensing & Commercial
  • Developer & Subdivision Bonds Municipal servicing, off-site works, and landscaping guarantees.
  • Electrical & Gas Contractor Bonds Provincial trade licensing and Technical Safety BC compliance bonds.
  • Administrative & License Bonds Permits, customs, freight, and regulatory compliance bonds for BC businesses.

In Canada, construction bond premiums typically range from 1% to 2% of the total contract value, depending on your company’s financial strength, project size, and bond type.
