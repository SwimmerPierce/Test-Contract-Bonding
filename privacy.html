Privacy Policy

How Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects commercial surety inquiry and underwriting information under the British Columbia and Alberta Personal Information Protection Acts (BC & AB PIPA) and Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. Who We Are (Operator Identification)

contractbonding.ca is a commercial and construction surety bond portal operated under exclusive license by Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.

  • Head Office: #101 – 4268 Lozells Avenue, Burnaby, BC V5A 0C6
  • Telephone: 604-420-2501
  • Business Hours: Monday – Friday, 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM PT
  • Licensing Scope: Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. is licensed in British Columbia and Alberta. Inquiries for businesses or projects located outside BC and Alberta are handled strictly by referral to an appropriately licensed partner brokerage with the applicant’s explicit consent.

2. Personal & Business Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry, surety bond application, or checklist download request on contractbonding.ca, we collect only the information reasonably necessary to evaluate your request and meet regulatory obligations:

  • Contact Information: Full name, company or legal business name, work email address, and telephone number.
  • Business & Underwriting Details: City, province/territory, years in business, business structure (Corporation, Partnership, or Sole Proprietorship), bond category, bond or contract amount, obligee/project owner, required-by date, CPA financial statement readiness, prior surety company status, and project scope notes.
  • Consent & Compliance Metadata: Privacy and referral consent acknowledgment, optional commercial insurance cross-sell request flag, unbundled CASL express marketing opt-in status, ISO consent timestamp, and CASL 6-month inquiry implied consent expiry tracking.
  • Technical & Security Logs: Standard server/edge logs, privacy-friendly web analytics (@vercel/analytics), and IP rate-limiting security logs on administrative authentication endpoints (/api/auth) to prevent unauthorized access.

3. Purposes for Collection, Use & Disclosure (BC PIPA s. 6, 7, 10)

We collect, use, and disclose your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Assessing preliminary surety bonding eligibility and underwriting readiness.
  • Obtaining surety bond quotes, Bid Bonds, Consents of Surety, and bonding facilities from licensed Canadian surety insurers and Managing General Agents (MGAs).
  • Quoting or reviewing Commercial General Liability (CGL), Builders Risk, Tools, or Commercial Auto insurance only if you request it via the optional cross-sell checkbox or a direct broker request.
  • Referring out-of-province inquiries (outside British Columbia and Alberta) to an appropriately licensed partner brokerage in your jurisdiction only with your express consent.
  • Responding to your inquiry (under CASL 6-month inquiry implied consent) and sending educational bonding guides or tender updates only if you expressly opt in via the unbundled CASL marketing checkbox.

4. Disclosure of Foreign / Cross-Border Service Providers (AB PIPA s. 13.1 & s. 6(2))

To operate this website and store online lead submissions, Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. uses third-party cloud infrastructure and email service providers—including Vercel Inc. (website hosting and global edge network), Neon Inc. / AWS (database hosting), and transactional email providers (such as Resend). Primary serverless execution (yul1 — Montréal, Canada) and production database hosting (aws-ca-central-1 — Montréal, Canada) are configured for Canadian regions where supported; however, certain operational logs, backups, edge routing, or support processing may occur outside Canada, including in the United States. Information processed outside Canada may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction, including lawful access by foreign courts, law enforcement, or national security authorities. Nexus maintains contractual and technical safeguards (PIPA s. 34) to protect your information.

5. Data Retention, Safeguards & Access / Correction Rights (PIPA s. 23, 34, 35)

  • Retention: Commercial insurance and surety brokerage records are retained for a minimum of 7 years (or longer where required by statutory limitation periods, surety indemnity obligations, or Insurance Council rules). Unconverted website inquiries are tracked for CASL 6-month implied consent expiry so marketing communications are not sent after expiry without express opt-in.
  • Safeguards: All web traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Lead records are stored in access-controlled databases accessible only through password-protected administrative credentials with 12-hour session expiry, IP brute-force rate limiting, and broker supervision audit fields.
  • Your Rights: Subject to statutory and contractual retention requirements, you have the right to request access to your personal information, request corrections to inaccurate information, or withdraw consent for optional uses. You may also unsubscribe from any CASL marketing emails at any time via a single-click unsubscribe link or by contacting our Privacy Officer.
