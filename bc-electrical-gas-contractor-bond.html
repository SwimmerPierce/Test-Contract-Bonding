BC Regulatory Compliance

Who Needs a Technical Safety BC Contractor Bond?

Technical Safety BC requires a continuous $10,000 surety bond as a condition of obtaining or renewing an electrical or gas contractor licence in British Columbia. The bond protects consumers if a contractor refuses or neglects to correct non-compliant work noted on a Certificate of Inspection.

01

Electrical Contractors

Class A, Class B, Class C, and restricted electrical contractors applying for or renewing a TSBC Electrical Contractor Licence alongside a designated Field Safety Representative (FSR).

02

Gas Contractors

Class A and Class B gas fitting contractors installing, altering, or servicing gas systems and appliances across residential, commercial, and industrial sites in BC.

03

Exact Obligee & Entity Match

Your bond must name British Columbia Safety Authority as the obligee and match your exact registered legal business name (or sole proprietor name) in the BC Business Registry.

Pricing & Express Track

How Much Does a $10,000 TSBC Bond Cost?

While the statutory bond penalty is $10,000 CAD, surety companies apply a minimum annual premium on small licence bonds rather than simple percentage math. For qualified BC electrical and gas contractors, annual premiums typically start around $250 to $350+ CAD per year, depending on credit history and insurer minimums.

What You Need to Apply (Express Track):

  • Exact legal business name (or sole proprietor legal name) matching your BC Business Registry & TSBC account.
  • Principal owner’s personal details for a standard credit check.
  • Active or pending Commercial General Liability (CGL) insurance details.
Important Notice: Submitting an online application does not bind, issue, or guarantee a surety bond. Coverage becomes active only after underwriting approval, payment, and formal execution by an authorized surety.

Common Questions

Technical Safety BC Bond FAQ

How fast can I get a Technical Safety BC $10,000 electrical or gas bond?

Most $10,000 TSBC electrical and gas contractor bonds are underwritten and issued within 24 to 48 business hours after we receive your completed application and credit authorization.

Do I need accountant-prepared financial statements for a $10,000 TSBC bond?

Typically no. Small statutory licence bonds are underwritten on an express track based on the owner’s personal credit profile and clean licensing history, without requiring Compilation or Review Engagement financial statements.

Does TSBC accept a digital PDF bond, or do I need a paper original?

Technical Safety BC accepts electronic bonds (e-bonds) or clear PDF scans of executed bonds uploaded directly through the Technical Safety BC Online Services portal. Our brokers confirm current portal formatting at issuance.

Can I upgrade from a $10,000 licence bond to Bid and Performance bonds later?

Yes. When you are ready to bid larger public or commercial electrical/mechanical tenders, Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. can help you set up a Bid Bond and Performance & L&M Payment Bond facility. See our First-Time Contractor Bonding Guide for the full roadmap.
