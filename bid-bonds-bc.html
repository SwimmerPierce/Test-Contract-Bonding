Tender Security Explained

Bid Bond vs. Consent of Surety (Agreement to Bond)

Most BC Bid, Metro Vancouver, TransLink, School District, Health Authority, and City of Calgary/Edmonton tenders require both documents in your bid package. Omitting either instrument can result in immediate tender disqualification.

01

Bid Bond (Usually 10% of Bid)

Typically issued on standard CCDC 220 wording, a Bid Bond guarantees that your bid was submitted in good faith and that, if awarded within the validity period (commonly 60 days), you will sign the contract and post the required final bonds.

02

Consent of Surety (Agreement to Bond)

Executed solely by the surety company, this companion letter commits the surety to issue your final Performance Bond (typically 50%) and Labour & Material Payment Bond (typically 50%) if your firm wins the contract.

03

Exact Tender Compliance

We verify the exact obligee name, tender/RFP reference number, percentage (10% Bid / 50% Performance / 50% L&M), and validity period so your submission meets public procurement rules.

Electronic Bonding

Digital E-Bonds & Turnaround Timelines

Modern procurement portals—including BC Bid, Bonfire, Merx, bids&tenders, and Alberta Purchasing Connection (APC)—accept cryptographically sealed digital E-Bonds with verifiable PKI signatures.

Critical E-Bond Upload Rule: Always upload the original, unflattened PDF file delivered by your surety broker. Never print, hand-sign, and scan an E-Bond, as doing so breaks the cryptographic certificate and strips the digital seal.

Underwriting & Pricing

What Underwriters Review Before Issuing a Bid Bond

Because a Consent of Surety commits the bonding company to support your final Performance and L&M Payment bonds upon award, underwriters pre-qualify your firm against the 3 C’s of Surety (Character, Capacity, Capital):

  • CPA Financial Statements: Compilation (Notice to Reader) for emerging facilities (~$250K–$500K); CPA Review Engagement for $500K–$2M+ tenders.
  • Work-in-Progress (WIP) Schedule: Current uncompleted backlog and gross margins.
  • 48-Hour Tender Warning: Apply at least 10–14 days before your first tender closing, or call 604-420-2501 immediately for urgent deadlines.

Common Questions

Bid Bond & Consent of Surety FAQ

Does submitting a Bid Bond application guarantee issuance before tender closing?

No. Submitting an online form does not bind, issue, or guarantee any Bid Bond or Consent of Surety. Coverage is never in effect until underwritten and formally executed by an authorized Attorney-in-Fact. For tenders closing within 48 hours, call 604-420-2501 directly.

What happens if I win a tender with a Bid Bond?

You convert your tender commitment into the required final contract bonds—usually a 50% CCDC 221 Performance Bond and CCDC 222 Labour & Material Payment Bond. Bid Bond fees on active annual facilities are typically nominal or $0 compared to the final contract bond premium.

Are digital E-Bonds accepted on BC Bid and municipal tenders?

Yes. BC Bid, Bonfire, Merx, bids&tenders, and Alberta Purchasing Connection (APC) accept verifiable digital E-Bonds issued through Surety Association of Canada recognized platforms. Upload the original digital PDF directly without printing or re-scanning.
