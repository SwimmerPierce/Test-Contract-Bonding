Standard Canadian Forms

CCDC 221 Performance Bond vs. CCDC 222 L&M Payment Bond

Most public infrastructure and commercial contracts in BC and Alberta specify a “50/50” final bond package (50% Performance + 50% L&M Payment), though certain private owners or lenders mandate 100% bond limits.

01

Performance Bond (CCDC 221)

Commonly issued at 50% or 100% of the contract price, protecting the Obligee (owner or prime GC) against contractor default, abandonment, or defective work. Includes a standard warranty/maintenance period (typically 12 months post-substantial performance).

02

L&M Payment Bond (CCDC 222)

Commonly issued at 50% of the contract price, protecting eligible first-tier subcontractors and material suppliers with a direct contract with the bonded Principal—especially vital on public projects where liens cannot be registered under BC’s Builders Lien Act or Alberta’s Prompt Payment and Construction Lien Act.

03

Subcontractor Default Protection

General contractors increasingly require 50%/50% or 100% Performance & L&M bonds from major mechanical, electrical, structural steel, and civil subtrades to protect critical-path schedules.

Pricing & Rating

Performance & Payment Bond Pricing Explained

For qualified contractors, combined Performance and L&M Payment bond rates typically range from 1% to 1.5% of the total contract price (subject to a $500+ minimum premium floor; higher for first-time or stretched facilities, and lower sliding-scale tiers for large established accounts).

Important Rating Clarification: In Canadian surety markets, bond premiums are calculated on the total contract price—not just the 50% bond penalty percentage. A 50% Performance Bond is not half the price of the contract rate.

Extended maintenance/warranty periods (beyond the standard 1-year period) or 100% bond penalties may carry additional underwriting charges.

Underwriting Readiness

The 6-Item Readiness Packet Underwriters Request

Having these six items organized speeds up approval for CCDC 221 and CCDC 222 final contract bonds.

1. Contractor Questionnaire

Company background, ownership structure, key estimators/superintendents, and largest completed projects.

2. 2–3 Years CPA Financials

Compilation (Notice to Reader) for ~$250K–$500K facilities; independent CPA Review Engagement for $500K–$2M+.

3. Current WIP Schedule

Work-in-Progress breakdown showing contract value, billed-to-date, cost-to-complete, and gross margin.

4. Shareholder PNW

Personal Net Worth statements for shareholders holding 10%+ equity to support the General Indemnity Agreement.

5. Bank Operating Line

Confirmation of your commercial credit facility limit, current utilization, and banking relationship.

6. Certificate of Insurance

Proof of active Commercial General Liability (CGL) and Builders Risk / Course of Construction coverage.

Common Questions

Performance & L&M Payment Bond FAQ

Is a 50% Performance Bond half the price of a 100% bond?

No. In Canada, surety rates are applied to the total contract price, not the 50% bond penalty percentage. A 100% Performance Bond generally requires stronger financial capacity and carries a higher rate than a standard 50%/50% package.

Do I need Review Engagement financials for a $1M+ Performance Bond?

Generally yes. While Compilation (Notice to Reader) statements often support emerging facilities up to ~$250,000–$500,000, $1M+ public and commercial Performance Bonds typically require independent CPA Review Engagement statements and a current WIP schedule.
