Bank Letter of Credit vs. Subdivision Surety Bond

Major BC and Alberta municipalities—including Vancouver, Burnaby, Calgary, and Edmonton—have modernized servicing agreements to permit on-demand surety bonds from approved, highly rated Canadian insurers.

Feature Irrevocable Bank Letter of Credit (LOC) Municipal Subdivision Surety Bond
Bank Credit Line Impact Dollar-for-dollar reduction in borrowing capacity (100% collateralized). Preserves bank operating and construction credit lines for active projects.
Annual Cost Bank standby fees (1%–2%) plus the opportunity cost of trapped cash/equity. Typical indicative rate 1.25%–2%/yr ($350+ min) depending on balance sheet.
Municipal Acceptance Accepted universally by municipalities. Accepted by many BC & AB municipalities (subject to municipal bylaw, credit rating, and exact template wording).
Best Use Cases Short-term deposits where surety is not permitted by local bylaw. Off-site civil servicing, roads/water/sewer, landscaping, DCC instalments, and warranty holdbacks.

Developer Underwriting

What Underwriters Evaluate for Developer Bonds

Unlike standard contractor WIP underwriting, developer subdivision and servicing bonds are evaluated on the strength and liquidity of the development entity and sponsors:

  • Balance Sheet Liquidity: Cash, marketable securities, and unencumbered equity relative to total bonded obligations.
  • Project Pro-Forma & Financing: Committed construction financing, municipal servicing cost estimates, and equity-in-project.
  • Development Track Record: History of completed subdivisions, rezonings, and municipal servicing sign-offs.
  • Corporate & Personal Indemnity: Executed General Indemnity Agreement (GIA) by the developer entity and principals.
Important Notice: Submitting an online application does not bind, issue, or guarantee a surety bond. Municipal acceptance depends on the specific city’s bylaw and approved wording.

Common Questions

Developer & Subdivision Surety Bond FAQ

Do Metro Vancouver and Alberta municipalities accept Subdivision Surety Bonds instead of LOCs?

Many do—including Vancouver, Burnaby, Calgary, and Edmonton—subject to each municipality’s engineering/bylaw policy, minimum insurer credit rating requirements, and exact approved bond wording. Nexus verifies the municipality’s accepted form before application.

Can I replace an existing active Bank Letter of Credit mid-project?

Often yes. Where permitted by the municipality’s servicing or development agreement, the city can return your existing Bank Letter of Credit upon receiving and approving the replacement Subdivision Surety Bond.

Do developers need Wrap-Up Liability or Course of Construction (Builders Risk) insurance?

Yes. Most lenders and municipalities require Wrap-Up Liability and Course of Construction (Builders Risk) coverage. Check “Also review my Commercial Insurance” in our form to bundle your project insurance with Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd.
