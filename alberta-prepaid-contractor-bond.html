Consumer Protection Act Compliance

Who Needs an Alberta Prepaid Contracting Bond?

Taking residential deposits without an active Prepaid Contracting Business Licence and surety bond can render contracts unenforceable and expose your business to Service Alberta fines or enforcement action.

01

Renovators & General Contractors

Residential and commercial renovators, kitchen/bath remodelers, basement builders, and deck/fence contractors signing agreements at a client’s home or jobsite and collecting upfront deposits.

02

Exterior, Roofing & Trade Contractors

Roofers, siding and window installers, landscapers, asphalt/paving crews, and HVAC, plumbing, or electrical trades accepting progress payments prior to full completion.

03

$10,000 vs. $25,000+ Bond Limits

Most new applicants start with a $10,000 baseline surety bond, though Service Alberta’s Director may require $25,000, $50,000, or $75,000+ depending on contract volume, revenue, or corporate history.

Pricing & Courier Logistics

Common Questions

Alberta Prepaid Contracting Bond FAQ

When does Service Alberta require a Prepaid Contracting Business Licence Bond?

Under Alberta’s Consumer Protection Act, any business that solicits, negotiates, or concludes a construction, renovation, or maintenance contract away from its permanent place of business and accepts deposits or progress payments before work is complete must hold a Prepaid Contracting Business Licence backed by a surety bond.

How much does a $10,000 Service Alberta bond cost?

Because small licence bonds are subject to insurer minimum premiums (not 1% = $100), annual premiums typically range from $250 to $400+ CAD per year for a $10,000 bond, depending on credit and business history.

Does Nexus ship the original paper bond to Alberta?

Yes. Because Service Alberta requires an original wet-ink signed and witnessed paper bond, we coordinate fast courier shipping directly to your Alberta address so you can sign and forward it to Service Alberta’s Consumer Programs Branch in Edmonton.

Is Nexus licensed in Alberta, and can you also arrange Bid or Performance bonds?

Yes. Nexus Insurance Brokers Ltd. is licensed in BC and Alberta, with Alberta accounts serviced by an individually AIC-licensed broker. As your business grows into commercial or municipal tenders, see our Calgary, Edmonton & Alberta Surety Bonds page or First-Time Contractor Bonding Guide.
